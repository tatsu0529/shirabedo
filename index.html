Shirabedo Uploader

Shirabedo Uploader is a personal tool used only by the creator of the "Zatsugaku Shirabedo" (雑学しらべ堂) channel to upload and schedule their own original short videos to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the general public.

雑学しらべ堂の運営者が、自分のTikTokアカウントに自分のショート動画を投稿・予約するための個人用ツールです。
