Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

1. About this app

Shirabedo Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool used only by the creator of the "Zatsugaku Shirabedo" channel to upload and schedule their own short videos to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Information we collect

Through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token and basic account information (user.info.basic: open ID, display name and avatar) for the account owner's TikTok account. The App does not collect information from any other TikTok users.

3. How we use information

The information is used only to upload videos and their captions and settings to the account owner's own TikTok account (video.upload, video.publish), and to confirm which account is connected. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or sale.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share any information with third parties.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only in a private environment that only the account owner can access, and are never published.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.

7. TikTok policies

Use of TikTok is governed by the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.
